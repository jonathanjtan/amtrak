      What to watch for

      The Zephyr's scenery, in order, with which way to look. Sides are for your eastbound train. The glass-roofed Sightseer Lounge car sees both sides.

      The teal eye marks these on the map and timeline above. Cardinal directions are fixed to the landscape; left / right assume you're facing forward on eastbound Train 6. California State Railroad Museum volunteers often narrate the Sierra and the Colorado Rockies stretches.

      Dining & the observation car

      Sightseer Lounge open to all

      The observation car. An upper deck walled in floor-to-ceiling windows, open to every passenger, with the Café on the lower level. It fills through the Rockies, so claim a spot before Denver.

      Dining car sleeper included

      Traditional Dining: chef-prepared breakfast and lunch and a three-course dinner, at communal tables of four. Complimentary for sleeping-car passengers; dinner is by reservation (your car attendant sets it up). Coach can join first-come if seats are free.

      Café car open to all

      Lower level of the lounge. Hot and cold snacks, coffee and drinks for sale through the day, and the only hot-meal option in coach. You can also bring your own food and eat it upstairs.
